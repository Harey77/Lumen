# User journeys

## J1. First visit, the core loop (persona: career-switching adult on a laptop)
1. **Today**: the headline asks "How does a cell turn food into energy?" One ink window offers *Start the mitochondrion*. The storage notice explains what is kept on the device.
2. **Mode chooser**: "Listen and watch" (Guided) or "Read and watch" (Reader).
3. **Explainer**: eight steps. The learner taps parts to read their role, rotates the model, and sees an analogy with its breaking point.
4. **Recall** after the cristae and ATP synthase steps: "Tap the part where ATP is made." Feedback names what was tapped.
5. **Recap quiz**: place six parts. Missed ones go to the Library review list.
6. **Skills**: the galaxy shows which parts were explored and which were checked in the app.

## J2. Struggle and adaptation (persona: rural student on a phone)
1. On the ATP synthase step the learner taps the wrong part three times.
2. Within 2 s an inline notice reads "Simplified: 3 missed taps in a row on this step." Labels drop to the current part, the analogy layer hides, the step splits into short scaffold cards, and narration slows.
3. **Revert** restores the full view in one tap. Settings shows the signals and lets the learner switch adaptation off.

## J3. Argue the losing side (Perspectives)
1. Pre-belief question.
2. Role brief: a 1950s biochemist expecting a chemical intermediate, which was a reasonable view given glycolysis.
3. Write the strongest argument (validated, saved locally), then predict the result of a leaky-membrane experiment.
4. Reveal: Mitchell's chemiosmotic hypothesis, the experiments that settled it, and the learner's own answers shown alongside.
5. Post-belief check. Belief revision is recorded as skill evidence.

## J4. Offline return
Close the tab, turn off the network and reopen. The app shell, fonts, explainer and saved progress all load. A header label says "Offline".

## J5. Ownership
Settings: export the learning record as JSON, see what is stored and roughly how much, and reset progress or clear everything (with confirmation).
